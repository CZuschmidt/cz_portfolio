## Overview

I am designing a custom flight computer PCB for the High-Altitude Balloon
team's payload. The board collects and logs data from every payload sensor so
the flight can be reconstructed afterwards.

## Sensors

- 9-DOF IMU
- GPS
- Pressure
- Temperature
- Humidity
- UV

## Purpose

The logged data is used for post-flight trajectory reconstruction and for
analyzing how weather shaped the balloon's trajectory.
