{%- comment -%} Sets `projects` to all projects in display order: 1. projects with an `order:` value, lowest number first 2. every other project, newest `date` first Used by the home page grid and the previous/next links on project pages. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign by_date = site.projects | sort: "date" | reverse -%} {%- assign ordered = by_date | where_exp: "p", "p.order" | sort: "order" -%} {%- assign unordered = by_date | where_exp: "p", "p.order == nil" -%} {%- assign projects = ordered | concat: unordered -%}
